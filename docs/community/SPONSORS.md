# Sponsoring ZCL Teleport

ZCL Teleport is free software (GPL-3.0-or-later). It has no ads, no accounts and no telemetry, and it stays that way. Sponsorship is entirely optional.

## How to sponsor

- **GitHub Sponsors:** https://github.com/sponsors/tungkernel (monthly or one-time). You can also use the **Sponsor** button at the top of the repository.

Sponsorship payments go to the maintainer, Nguyễn Hoàng Tùng ([@tungkernel](https://github.com/tungkernel)).

## What your support covers

- Maintainer time for fixes, new releases and answering issues.
- Test machines and OS licenses for platforms the maintainer does not own (macOS, Linux, older Windows builds).
- Hosting and domain costs for the project website, if any appear.

## What sponsoring does not change

- Every feature stays available to everyone. No feature is paywalled or removed for people who do not sponsor.
- The app never asks for money and never shows donation prompts.
- Sponsors get no special access to user data, because the project does not collect any.
